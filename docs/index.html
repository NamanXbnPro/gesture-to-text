How it works

No neural network guesses the word. A hand tracker finds 21 points on your hand, and simple geometry decides which fingers are up.

1

Find the hand

Google’s MediaPipe Hand Landmarker locates 21 joints per hand in every video frame, for up to two hands.

2

Check each finger

A finger counts as up only when it is straight and reaching away from the wrist. The thumb is judged against the index knuckle, so a rotated hand still works.

3

Match one pose exactly

A word shows only when the finger pattern matches a pose exactly and holds for a moment. A half-formed pose stays a dash, so it never gets the wrong word.

Help

The camera will not start

Allow the camera in your browser’s site settings (the lock icon by the address). Close other apps that use the camera, and make sure the page is open over https.

My pose is not recognised

Keep your whole hand in view with the palm toward the camera, in good light. Curl unused fingers all the way in, and hold still for about a third of a second.

There is no sound

Turn on Speech for that hand and check your device volume. Speech uses the voice built into your browser, so no account is needed.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Frames are processed on your device. Only the hand model and library files are downloaded when the page loads.
